Returns & exchanges.

Every pair is one of one and described honestly. This page says exactly when we take a pair back, and when we can't.

When we accept a return or exchange

We accept a return or exchange only if the pair you receive is significantly different from the pair shown in our photos and listing. That means:

  • You receive a different pair, model or colour from the one you ordered.
  • The pair has a defect that the listing and photos do not show or describe, such as a sole that is separating or a broken zip or buckle.
  • The pair is in a clearly worse condition than the grade and photos show.

Small differences are not significant. This includes lighting or colour shifts in photos, and marks that are visible in the photos or described in the grade.

How to make a claim

  • Message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery. Claims after this window are not accepted.
  • Do not wear, clean, repair or alter the pair before we have reviewed your claim.
  • Send an unboxing video that shows the parcel sealed, the opening, the pair's label, and the problem. Keep it uncut.
  • Send photos of the pair next to the listing photos.

We will review your claim and reply within 2 working days.

What is not covered

  • Change of mind, or a pair you no longer want.
  • Size or fit. All sizes are in EUR please ask if for other measuring systems for better understanding.
  • Wear, scuffs, marks or smell that the grade and photos describe.
  • Aftermarket laces. Some pairs include replacement laces that are not the original. This is disclosed and is not a defect or a reason to return.
  • Damage after delivery, including from wearing, cleaning, repair or alteration.
  • Claims made after 48 hours, or without the unboxing video described above.
Prices are fixed and non-negotiable. A return or exchange under this page is the only way a completed sale is reversed.
