Privacy policy.

What we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it. Last updated [jo bhi date].

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, delivery address, phone number, the pair(s) you ordered, and your payment proof (for example, a transfer screenshot).
  • When you message us: the contents of your WhatsApp conversation with us, including any photos or videos you send for a claim.
  • When you browse: our site does not use advertising trackers or analytics. Your bag is stored only in your own browser on your device, and is not sent to us until you message us.
  • Our site loads fonts from Google. Google may receive your IP address when fonts load, under Google's own privacy policy.

Why we use it

We use your information to confirm and dispatch your order, take payment, arrange delivery, handle returns and exchanges, and answer your messages. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it.

Who we share it with

  • Courier: Who receive your name, address and phone number to deliver your parcel.
  • Payment: Bank / JazzCash / Easypaisa / Sadapay / Nayapay, only to confirm the payment you make.
  • We share information only when the law requires it, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

How long we keep it

We keep order and payment records for four years for accounting, dispute handling and returns. Chat records are kept for four years unless a claim is open. We delete or anonymise information once it is no longer needed, except where the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Message us on WhatsApp at +92 335 9189183. We will reply within 48 hours. We may keep certain records, such as completed orders and payment records, where the law or our returns policy requires it.

Keeping your information safe

Only people who run Kopp and need your details to do their job can see them. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee it. We will never ask you for your bank PIN, card PIN or account password. If someone asks you for these in Kopp's name, do not share them and tell us.

Children

Kopp is for adults who buy shoes for themselves. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
