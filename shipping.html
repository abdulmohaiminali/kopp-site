Shipping & ordering.

We deliver anywhere in Pakistan. Here is how an order moves from your message to your door.

How to order

  • Choose your pair(s) on the site, then message any Kopp admin on WhatsApp to confirm they are available.
  • Pay the full price of the pair(s) in advance, by Bank transfer/ Nayapay / JazzCash / Easypaisa / Sadapay. Payment details are sent on WhatsApp.
  • Orders are processed on a first-payment, first-served basis. A pair is reserved for you only when your payment is received.
  • Prices are fixed and non-negotiable.

Delivery time

Delivery takes approximately 3 to 4 working days from dispatch. Working days exclude Sundays and public holidays. These are estimates, not guarantees. Courier delays caused by weather, strikes, or remote locations are outside our control, and we will keep you updated on WhatsApp.

We share your courier tracking number on WhatsApp when your order is dispatched.
