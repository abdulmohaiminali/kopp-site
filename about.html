Why Kopp?

Kopp finds good shoes that still have years left in them, cleans them up, and sends them to you anywhere in Pakistan. Every pair is one of one. When it is gone, it is gone.

How we grade

A+ is close to new. A shows light wear. B shows visible wear, and we photograph every flaw so there are no surprises.

How we prepare

Each pair is sterilized thoroughly before it is photographed, so what you see is what arrives.

How to order

Add pairs to your bag and send the order on WhatsApp. We confirm payment and delivery with you there.
